# Play-ready screenshots (VST-61)

Phone screenshots for the Google Play listing, made from the originals in `docs/store/screenshots/`.

- 1080x1920 px, 24-bit PNG without transparency (Google Play accepts 320 to 3840 px per side, long side at most 2x the short side). The originals are about 357x780 with an alpha channel, so they are not accepted as they are.
- Each original is flattened on the cream background, scaled to a height of 1720 px, rounded, and centred on the brand blue `#176AAB`.
- 5 screens per language (en, fr, ar): `01-login`, `02-search`, `03-details`, `04-details-scrolled`, `05-map`. Same Demo data as the originals, no personal data.
- The screenshots are scaled up about 2.4x, so they are slightly soft. Retake them at a higher resolution if sharper images are needed.

The feature graphic in `docs/brand/` now says "Buy, rent or find a stay in Tunisia", to match the short description.
